## XFiles 1.4.3-smb — Cast playback continuity and direction-selectable auto-advance

A signed stable update after `v1.4.2-smb`. This release fixes the Mobile Chromecast
controller returning to the folder's first video when the current receiver item finishes,
and lets users choose whether Cast should continue through the folder.

### Mobile Chromecast playback

- **Keep the selected video** after a single receiver item ends. The controller,
  browser selection and notification use the last confirmed receiver media identity
  instead of falling back to the first folder video when Cast clears its item.
- **Choose what happens after a video ends** from icon-and-label controls in the
  Chromecast player: **Stop (default)**, **Next**, or **Previous**.
- **Next / Previous** play the adjacent video in the current folder's playlist.
  At the first or last video, playback stops: **no wraparound or repeat**.
- The chosen mode stays active when the Cast controller is dismissed and reopened
  in the same application process. It resets to Stop on process restart.
- Only natural completion advances; pausing, manual stopping, disconnects, receiver
  errors and seek operations do not start another video. Repeated terminal events
  for one video are de-duplicated.
- The Cast receiver continues to receive **one media item at a time**. The local
  player and TV edition playback behavior are unchanged.

### Distribution and compatibility

- This version publishes distinct signed **Mobile** and **Google TV** APKs; the
  release CI also builds a Mobile AAB artifact.
- The existing single-channel `debug-latest` self-update contract introduced in
  1.4.2-smb is unchanged. The stable tagged APKs are installed manually from the
  GitHub Releases page; they are not a separate in-app Nightly or Normal channel.
- Android 8.0 (API 26) or later is required. Cast depends on a compatible receiver
  on a reachable local network.
- GitHub CI covers Mobile/TV unit tests, APK build, lint and Android emulator smoke
  tests. **Physical Chromecast end-of-video transitions and upgrades from installed
  stable/Debug builds have not been verified on hardware**; verify those cases on
  target devices before treating them as certified.
